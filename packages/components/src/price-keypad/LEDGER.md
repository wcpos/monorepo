# Behaviour ledger: `price-keypad`

Seeded 2026-10-24 with the component, from Paul's rulings of 2026-10-24 ("D is the only acceptable option": the cart price does what the quantity does, decision 36; "three quick keys is enough") and the price-keypad-variants board, variant 1. Numbers are assigned once and never reused: a struck line leaves a gap, a new line takes the next number. Every line keeps its evidence. The rules for preserving or striking a line are in the [library strategy](https://github.com/wcpos/roadmap/blob/worktree-docs%2Bdesign-program-2026-09-12/docs/design/2026-09-18-library-strategy.md), section 3.

**Job:** The cart's price (and regular price, fee, shipping) and, since 2026-10-27, the Products grid's regular, sale and cost prices on web: the number is the button, and it grows in place into a keypad with up to three quick discounts; the phone sheet below the phone width.

**Base:** `grow-keypad` (the popover, the grid, focus, the physical keyboard, Done, the sheet), `button`, `text`. Behaviour lives in the pure `reducer.ts` over `lib/decimal-entry`.
