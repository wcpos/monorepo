# Behaviour ledger: `pressable-surface`

Created 2026-10-28 by card 4 of the component-library review (`architecture-review-component-library-2026-10-28`, slice W1-B). Numbers are assigned once and never reused: a struck line leaves a gap, a new line takes the next number. Every line keeps its evidence.

**Job:** The one pressable surface under every row, tile, nav item and button: the role and what it announces, focusability, the press wash and its fine-pointer hover, the one disabled dim, the native haptic, Space on non-button roles and the 44 pt target. `ListRow`, `ListItem`, `Tile`, `MenuRow`, `NavItem`, `IconButton` and `Button` render on it and keep only their own layout.

**Not its job:** a layout. Geometry, borders, radius and the selected paint stay with the layout (`list-row` flush and ruled, `list-item` and `tile` on `TILE_SURFACE`, `menu-row` the overlay row, `nav-item` the lift on the ground).

**Base:** React Native Pressable, `usePointer` (`lib/device`), `hapticPress` (`lib/haptics`), `MinTargetBand` (`lib/min-target`). No platform split.

## Lines

1. Unset, a surface with `onPress` is a `button`; a named role (`role`, or the legacy `accessibilityRole`) wins. A surface with no action and no role has no role and is no tab stop (`tabIndex={-1}`: react-native-web's Pressable ignores `focusable`); an action, a long press or a named role keeps it a tab stop, so a `Button` in an `asChild` menu trigger (whose press comes from Radix, not `onPress`) stays reachable — evidence: `list-row` line 6, `list-item` line 3 and `tile` line 7, the three copies this replaced (CodeRabbit on #2640, Codex on #2641); test `pressable-surface.test.tsx`.
2. `selected` is announced, not only painted: `aria-checked` on `radio`, `checkbox`, `menuitemradio` and `menuitemcheckbox`, `aria-selected` on every other role; React Native maps both onto the state a native screen reader reads. A caller's own `aria-checked` wins. `ListItem` painted `selected` without announcing it until it moved here — evidence: `list-row` line 4, `tile` lines 3 and 7, `menu-row` line 3; report card 4 and appendix B ("ListItem likewise paints selected without aria-selected"); test (role × selected matrix).
3. The wash: `muted` (default) presses on `active:bg-muted` and hovers on `web:hover:bg-muted` on a fine pointer only (a coarse pointer keeps a hover after the tap); `card` is the nav lift, `destructive` the `bg-destructive/10` row wash, `none` leaves it to the caller (`Button` and `IconButton`, whose variants carry their own). Only a washed surface reads the pointer, so a Button never calls `usePointer` — evidence: `list-row` line 3, `nav-item` line 1, `menu-row` lines 2 and 5; test (wash table).
4. One disabled dim: `opacity-45` and `web:pointer-events-none`, no press, and `disabled` always a real boolean (an undefined one after a disabled render latches the Android view at enabled=false, `button` line 1). `dimmed` dims the same and still presses (`MenuRow locked`). The value is the controls tier's (wiki `component-library` "disabled is `opacity-45` across the tier", 21 folders); `ListRow` and `TILE_SURFACE` were `opacity-50` and now match — evidence: `list-row` line 5, `tile` line 6, `menu-row` lines 5 and 9; test.
5. Every enabled press gives the light impact haptic on native (`hapticPress`; never on the web, never when disabled); `haptic={false}` opts out. It was inlined in `Tile`, `MenuRow`, `Button`, `IconButton` and `Tabs` while `lib/haptics` sat unused, and `ListRow`, `ListItem` and `NavItem` gave none; they all give it now — evidence: `tile` line 8, `menu-row` line 8, `button` line 13, `icon-button` line 4; test.
6. react-native-web presses on Space only for the `button` role; a `radio`, `checkbox`, `option`, `menuitem*` or `tab` surface presses on Space here, after the caller's `onKeyDown`, unless that claimed the key or the key came from a control nested inside (a row's remove button) — evidence: `menu-row` line 7 (the choice-row handler this generalises), `list-item` line 3 (a checkbox row needed Space); test.
7. `minTarget` adds `MinTargetBand` as the last child, the one 44 pt mechanism (`lib/min-target.tsx`): a transparent band the control token tall, centred and overflowing, out of layout. It replaces the measured `useMinTargetSlop` (sm Button, sm Chip), the static `hitSlop` 12 (Checkbox, RadioGroupItem, xs IconButton), 8 (sm IconButton, the chip's clear control) and 4 (the tree toggle). Why the band and not the slop: iOS Fabric hit-tests nothing outside a parent that no child overflows (so slop past a 24 caption row never reached the button) and React Native Web ignores `hitSlop`, so slop was 44 on native inside a 44 row only and never on touch web; a real overflowing child is hit-tested on iOS and Android and is part of the control's DOM on the web, the argument `icon-button` line 7 made — evidence: `icon-button` line 7, `switch` line 5 (the same construction), `button` line 19, `chip` line 13; tests `pressable-surface.test.tsx`, `lib/min-target.test.tsx`.
8. `primitiveKeys`: a primitive's item around the surface (the anchored dropdown's Radix items through `asChild`) already presses on Enter and Space at keydown, so the surface marks the key handled before the chain runs (Radix's composed handler then skips its click, and line 6's Space press skips) and ignores react-native-web's keyup press. Without it an anchored item pressed twice on Space and Enter, three times on Enter when it stays open, and a checkbox item toggled back — evidence: card 3 follow-up on #3006; test `dropdown-menu/keys.web.test.tsx` (2/2/2/3 presses and 2/3 toggles before, 1 each after).
