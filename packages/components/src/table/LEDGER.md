10. The row's `min-h-row` floor sits on an inner wrapper, the outer view is `min-h-0`, and the natural state is written explicitly (`height: 'auto'`, `overflow: 'visible'`) because Reanimated merges style updates: a row restored after a failed write gets its height back, not a held `height: 0`.
