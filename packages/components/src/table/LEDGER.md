9. A removal is tint, then the row's height closing to nothing (200 ms on the shared ease, from the height its last natural layout reported), then the mutation: the lines below rise into the gap before the data changes under them. `armRemove(on)` is the tint alone, for a swipe that has crossed the remove point and may come back; it is ignored while a removal is committing. Reduced motion, or a row never laid out, commits straight after the tint; a failed write restores the natural height — evidence: cart line swipe-to-remove, chosen 2026-10-08; tests `pulse-row.test.tsx`: “closes the gap after the tint and commits the removal only once it is shut”, “armRemove turns the removal tint on and off without committing anything”. Lines 6–7 (the latch) unchanged.
10. The row's `min-h-row` floor sits on an inner wrapper, the outer view is `min-h-0`, and the natural state is written explicitly (`height: 'auto'`, `overflow: 'visible'`) because Reanimated merges style updates: a row restored after a failed write gets its height back, not a held `height: 0`. An add pulse cancels the close as well as the tint, and a cancelled removal calls `pulseRemove`'s `onCancel` so a caller holding the row off stage brings it back; a failed write also drops the tint. An armed row (`armRemove(true)`) skips the tint timing and closes at once — evidence: Codex review of #2447, four findings, 2026-10-08; tests `pulse-row.test.tsx` “cancellation while the row is on its way out”.
