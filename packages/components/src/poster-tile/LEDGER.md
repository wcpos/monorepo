# Behaviour ledger: `poster-tile`

Seeded 2026-10-12 with the component, from the Poster spec (wcpos/roadmap `docs/specs/2026-10-12-pos-product-tile-poster-spec.md`, #424 + #425) and its prototype (`docs/prototypes/2026-10-12-pos-product-tile-poster/index.html`). Numbers are assigned once and never reused: a struck line leaves a gap, a new line takes the next number. Every line keeps its evidence.

**Job:** The register's product grid tile: the picture is the whole square and the words sit on a card-colour scrim at its foot. It knows nothing about products: a picture, marks and words.

**Base:** `react-native` Pressable and View, `react-native-reanimated` (the stack's style only), `react-native-svg` for the native scrim and `@react-native-masked-view/masked-view` (0.3.2, already in the binary through expo-router; pinned directly) for its blur's mask, a CSS gradient and `backdrop-filter` on web; `Image`, `Icon`, `Text`.

## Lines

1. The scrim is the card colour at 92 %, eased in over 48 px above the first line of words and flat under them, thirteen stops (`SCRIM_STOPS`); reach and strength are constants, not settings — evidence: owner decision 4, 2026-10-12 ("card colour, eased, 85, 48"), spec § The scrim; raised to 92 by the owner on 2026-10-13 ("around 90 or 92") after 85 read thin over busy pictures on dev-next.
2. No scrim is drawn when every word slot is empty; the marks still show — evidence: spec § The scrim; test: `poster-tile.test.tsx`.
3. A tint square carries its words directly, with no scrim; `picture.tint` is the token number `bg-tile-tint-N`, and `tintOf` is the one place the rule picking it lives — evidence: spec § No picture. `picture.kind = 'fill'` is the accent fill (`bg-primary`, `primary-foreground` words and glyph), used by the All products tile only — evidence: owner 2026-10-29, board 2 frame L.
4. The frame keeps `m-1`, `grow` / `flex-1`, `lifted` (opacity 0) and forwards its ref to the `Pressable`, so the Deal measures and lifts it as it did the old tiles — evidence: spec § Behaviour that must not move.
5. A stack's two cards (3 px and 6 px down-right) are drawn inside the frame, behind the card (square and band), and only the card clips; they take an animated style so the Deal can fade them — evidence: spec § The stack and the Deal, rules 1–3. Since 2026-10-13 every tile that deals is a stack: the drill variable product, every term tile (term, All products, shortcut) and the parent term in slot 0, whose cards fade with the Deal as the product parent's do; never a variation, a placeholder or the inline-style variable tile. The two kinds still read apart: a category keeps its folder, grid or sliders glyph and its "N products"; a variable product has a price, "from" and "N options" and no glyph — evidence: owner, 2026-10-13: "Any card that drills down into a new stack should have that consistent UI."
6. The press lays a `bg-foreground/10` veil over the square, under the words and marks, so a picture and a tint dim alike (a tint has no picture to fade); never a background change — evidence: spec § Geometry; review of monorepo#2485 (All products, shortcuts and photoless products showed nothing on tap).
7. A count mark is the number alone in a card pill (warning when low); its words are its accessibility label — evidence: owner decision 5, spec § Marks.
8. `picture.kind = 'slot'` lets the caller render the picture itself. DEVIATION from the spec's three picture kinds, recorded: a product's picture needs its own Suspense boundary, attachment hook, render probes and the Deal's `useCopyPicture`, which belong to the products screen, not to this component — evidence: plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-12-pos-poster-tile.md`, Task 2.
9. `mark.kind = 'glyph'` (All products' grid, a shortcut's sliders) is a CLARIFICATION of the spec's marks; on a tint a folder or glyph sits in the tint without its circle, as the prototype draws it — evidence: plan Task 2; prototype `.fglyph`.
10. The words' block is never taller than the card and clips at its bottom, so on a narrow tile (8 columns) the facts go first and the name, money and sub stay — evidence: review of monorepo#2485; test: `poster-tile.test.tsx`.
11. On web the picture under the words is blurred (`SCRIM_BLUR_PX`, 6 px, a `backdrop-filter` layer under the words) and masked by the same thirteen stops, so the blur fades in exactly with the tint and has no edge. Native cannot mask a live blur, so it draws a blurred copy of the picture (line 14) under a `MaskedView` whose mask is the same stops as black at full alpha (`scrimStopsFor`, the one stop list for tint and mask) — evidence: owner on dev-next, 2026-10-13 (plateau "around 90 or 92" and a slight blur under the words); research: roadmap `docs/research/2026-10-13-native-scrim-blur.md` (Recommendation); tests: `poster-tile.test.tsx` (web blur layer, `scrimStopsFor`).
12. Every tile is the square and a band of the card under it (`POSTER_BAND`), so every card in a grid is the same height, picture or tint. The band holds one line of name and the money row (their line heights, the 2 px gap and the 10 px foot, in the type scale's variables, so it scales with the text); the words are anchored to the card's foot, so those two lines sit on the band, the fade begins at the picture's bottom edge, and any further line climbs into it. One hairline border wraps square and band; a tint fills both — evidence: owner, 2026-10-13, on the board `pos-tile-square-or-band-2026-10-13.html`: "I like the band" and "The cards must be the same height, square or rectangle."; tests: `poster-tile.test.tsx` (band, card anchoring).
13. On native the band follows the OS text size as `Text` does (capped at `MAX_FONT_SCALE`): `band.native.tsx` reads the type scale's variables with `useCSSVariable` and scales the two text lines by the font scale, the gap and foot not; web keeps the class. The marks, the cart count and the back badge paint after the words, at the card's top corners, so a fade and blur that climb far never wash over them. `PosterPlaceholder` is the tile's shape (square and band in `bg-muted`) for every slot that has not arrived and for the grid's loading rows — evidence: review of monorepo#2520; tests: `poster-tile.test.tsx` (mark order, placeholder).
14. The native blur is a twin of the picture: `blurred` on a `slot` picture is the slot owner's own blurred copy (the products screen's `TileImageBlurred`, the term tile's blurred image); for `image` and `mosaic` the tile builds it (the same markup with `blurRadius`, `transition: 0`, no callbacks, recycling key `<key>:blur`); a tint has none, and a slot without one draws the tint alone. The tile hands it to `Scrim` as `blur`, on a `bg-card` backing with the square's `out` greying and press veil, so it shows what web's `backdrop-filter` sees. Web ignores the prop and never mounts it. In the native scrim it paints first, under the tint and words, positioned `bottom` = the band's height (`usePosterBandHeight`) and `height` = the scrim's measured width, so it lines up with the square. Radius `SCRIM_BLUR_RADIUS`: iOS `SCRIM_BLUR_PX * 2` (SDImageBlurTransformer halves it), Android 2 (Glide blurs after a x4 downsample; judged on the Pixel 10 against no twin and radius 5: 2 softens the edge, 5 reads mushy). VERIFIED 2026-10-13 on the Pixel 10 (Android 17, RN 0.88: masked-view renders the gradient mask) and on an iPad simulator (iOS 26.5, a local source-built dev client: fade, blur and registration as on web) — evidence: research roadmap `docs/research/2026-10-13-native-scrim-blur.md`; device screenshots in monorepo#2531; test: `poster-tile.test.tsx` (never mounted on web).
15. The native scrim's SVGs take the scrim's MEASURED width and height as numbers, never `100%`: on Android react-native-svg drew a `height="100%"` Svg only as tall as the fade, so the tint stopped at the reach and the lowest strip of the square and the plateau were bare (every native scrim since #2485 had this; nobody had looked at native) — evidence: Pixel 10 screenshots in monorepo#2531 (with and without the twin, then sized), 2026-10-13.
16. `PosterThumb` (`thumb.tsx`) is the tile's vocabulary on a table row: a square of `size-14` (touch) or `size-10` (mouse), the picture or a tint, the folder / grid / sliders glyph in a 20 px card circle over a picture (`Mark` `compact`) and bare in a tint, the two stack cards (the tile's `NEAR` and `FAR`, now in `style.ts` with `CARD`, `CIRCLE` and `TINTS`) and the sold-out greying. No pressable and no veil: the row is the control. `Mark` lives in `mark.tsx` and `StockPill` (`stock-pill.tsx`, the count and backorder pills) is exported for the table's stock cells, so the tile and the rows draw one pill — evidence: roadmap `docs/specs/2026-10-14-pos-table-rows-spec.md`; tests: `poster-thumb.test.tsx` (bare folder in a tint, circle over a picture, two cards when stacked); `poster-tile.test.tsx` unchanged.
17. One ink: `PosterTile` and `PosterThumb` provide `text-primary-foreground` for a `fill` picture and nothing otherwise; `Mark` takes the provided ink when bare and stays muted when none is provided (a tint's bare glyph is unchanged); `StockPill` provides its tone to count and glyph together — tests: `poster-tile.test.tsx` filled words and mark, stock count and glyph; `poster-thumb.test.tsx` filled mark, muted bare glyph on a tint.
