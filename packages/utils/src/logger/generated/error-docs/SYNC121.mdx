## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your store cannot be reached right now, so changes will stay on this device.

Check the connection and keep working offline; syncing will retry automatically when the store is reachable.

## What to do {#what-to-do}

You can keep working — WCPOS handles this automatically. WCPOS retries this automatically.

## Your data {#your-data}

The change is saved on this device but has not reached your store.

## Details {#details}

- **Code:** `SYNC121` (`SYNC_UNREACHABLE`)
- **Severity:** warn
- **Introduced in:** WCPOS 1.10.0
