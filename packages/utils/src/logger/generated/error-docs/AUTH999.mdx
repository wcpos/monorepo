## What this means {#what-this-means}

Signing in or staying signed in hit an unexpected problem.

Try the action once more. If it repeats, sign out and back in, then export diagnostics and contact support.

## What to do {#what-to-do}

Try the action again. WCPOS does not retry this by itself — retry when you are ready.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order or product data is affected. If this persists, export diagnostics and contact WCPOS support.

## Details {#details}

- **Code:** `AUTH999` (`AUTH_UNEXPECTED`)
- **Severity:** error
- **Introduced in:** WCPOS 1.10.0
