## What this means {#what-this-means}

Your store sent a malformed response that WCPOS had to repair before reading.

This usually means a plugin or PHP notice is printing extra output into store responses. WCPOS recovered this one, but repairs are not guaranteed — ask the site administrator to check the site's error log.

## What to do {#what-to-do}

You can keep working — WCPOS handles this automatically. WCPOS retries this automatically.

## Your data {#your-data}

No order or product data is affected. If this persists, ask the person who manages your WordPress site.

## Details {#details}

- **Code:** `SYNC151` (`STORE_RESPONSE_MALFORMED`)
- **Severity:** warn
- **Introduced in:** WCPOS 1.10.0
