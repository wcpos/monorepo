## What this means {#what-this-means}

Checkout cannot continue because a product SKU is duplicated or invalid.

Change or remove the named SKU, then retry with the preserved cart.

## What to do {#what-to-do}

Correct the highlighted details, then try again. Retry after making the change above; retrying without it will fail the same way.

## Your data {#your-data}

The order itself is safe and unchanged.
