Seeded 2026-09-18 from `.claude/research/2026-09-18-composed-behaviour-ledger.md` on `research/composed-ledger` (wcpos/roadmap#341) by wcpos/roadmap#345. Numbers are assigned once and never reused: a struck line leaves a gap, a new line takes the next number. Every line keeps its evidence. The rules for preserving or striking a line are in the [library strategy](https://github.com/wcpos/roadmap/blob/worktree-docs%2Bdesign-program-2026-09-12/docs/design/2026-09-18-library-strategy.md), section 3. **Job:** Coordinate Sales and Closures, their scope controls, access rules and report data.

**Composes:** `@wcpos/components/{button,calendar,card,error-boundary,hstack,icon,panels,popover,suspense,tabs,text,vstack}`.

21. The till strip above the Sales body is outside the date: it reads the register session, movements, closures and expected drawer the Closures room reads and reads the server again every minute while the screen is focused and online, keeping what it shows until the answer arrives and retrying after a failure (its result chip is the hook's server-anchored expected, never a second derivation), never the reports query, and is the only way into the Closures room — evidence: build brief §1 “Till strip”; wcpos/roadmap#332; `reports/till-strip.tsx`, `till-strip.test.tsx` — platform: all.

22. Comparison is on by default and only changed: a day against yesterday or the same weekday last week, a week against the week before, a month against the month before, a live day at the same time of day; comparison orders come from a second binding on the same filters with the shifted range — evidence: build brief §2; wcpos/roadmap#332 PR 2a — platform: all.

23. The chart view (by hour/day or running total) is device state for the visit, never in the query — evidence: build brief §2; this PR.

24. The period section follows the date and computes every card on the device from selected orders (the status set and unticked orders already applied); the till strip stays outside — evidence: build brief §1 “Period section”, §2; wcpos/roadmap#332 PR 3a.
25. 31. Every panel prints through the plugin's `report` templates from a document built on the device from the panel's rows (`buildReportDocument`), only when the store, the panel's data and the templates are known and only through templates that can render locally; the on-screen rows stay the panel's own table — evidence: build brief §1 “Detail panels”, the plugin's `Report_Document_Validator`, wcpos/monorepo#2262's review, wcpos/roadmap#332 PR 3e — platform: all.

34. A panel whose rows miss a cost names the gap in its footer; a row with no cost shows dashes, never 100%. Partial rows report profit and margin only for costed sales — evidence: wcpos/roadmap#361 “costs unset”, this PR — platform: all.
35. Read refunds by the day they were made through the refunds lane, scoped to the room by their local parent order (including parents outside the sales period), or their own POS identity when the parent is not local. Only the printed refund row falls back to the embedded sum while period refunds are unknown. The hero stays gross sales — evidence: build brief §2, wcpos/roadmap#302, wcpos/monorepo#2277, this PR — platform: all.
36. Margin nets period refund lines into the same product/category/brand groupings: negative totals, negative costs and negative quantities. Missing-cost coverage counts absolute quantities on sale and refund lines; zero-net groups remain rows — evidence: wcpos/roadmap#361, wp-env proof on wcpos/roadmap#332 (`cost_of_goods_sold.value: −12.5`), this PR — platform: all.

37. Period refunds of a local non-POS parent join the room, still subject to cashier scope and selection — evidence: wcpos/roadmap#332 ruling A (2026-09-30), this PR — platform: all.
38. Where sold shows In store · Online by `created_via`, with Registers as its second view under All registers only; each view opens its own panel behind the head. The figure is the period total and the shares sum to it — evidence: wcpos/roadmap#332 (decided card; ruling A 2026-09-30), wcpos/monorepo#2254’s first head, this PR — platform: all.
