# Behaviour ledger: `pos/columns`

Seeded 2026-09-18 from `.claude/research/2026-09-18-composed-behaviour-ledger.md` on `research/composed-ledger` (wcpos/roadmap#341) by wcpos/roadmap#345. Numbers are assigned once and never reused: a struck line leaves a gap, a new line takes the next number. Every line keeps its evidence. The rules for preserving or striking a line are in the [library strategy](https://github.com/wcpos/roadmap/blob/worktree-docs%2Bdesign-program-2026-09-12/docs/design/2026-09-18-library-strategy.md), section 3. Not reworded from the source.

**Job:** Arrange extension-provided POS panels and replace only the products content during checkout.

**Composes:** `@wcpos/components/panels`.

## Lines

1. Use one slot-driven layout for both routes, reverse panel order with products position and persist the products panel’s width only after user resizing — route/side changes must not create separate layout behavior or save programmatic sizing — evidence: `a061650537 2026-09-07 feat(pos): one slot-driven columns layout for both POS routes; open-order tabs become the pos.cart.bar slot entry with a top/bottom setting` — platform: all, wide layout.
2. Supply complementary default sizes to both panels and a minimum size — an unsized cart can otherwise remain a roughly 1.5% sliver on slow Android renderers — evidence: `pos-columns.tsx:73–79`, “BOTH panels stay sized”; “products panel next to an unsized cart renders 60:1 — a ~1.5% cart sliver.”; “With both sides sized the fallback IS the” / “correct layout, so the race is harmless.” — platform: all; observed defect on Android.
3. Swap only products content, keep the cart present, key tender by order UUID and prefer the independently selected receipt — two orders must not share a keypad reducer, and a new draft must not displace the paid receipt — evidence: `pos-columns.tsx:98`, “Keyed by order: two orders in checkout must not share one keypad reducer.”; `da7341cd68 2026-09-07 feat(checkout): the tender pane replaces the products column in place; checkout becomes a mode of the order` — platform: all, wide layout.
4. Fade checkout content over 180 ms rather than slide/rise — preserve the motion selected in the prototype ruling — evidence: `pos-columns.tsx:18`, “Ruled on wcpos/roadmap#165 from the prototype comparison (fade vs rise/slide/instant).” — platform: all, wide layout.
5. Give slot consumers stable module-level side snapshots with no notifications during a panel’s mount — a fixed layout side must not create unstable external-store snapshots — evidence: `pos-columns.tsx:27–28`, “A panel's side is fixed for as long as it is mounted, so these views never notify.” / “They are module constants because `useSlotValue` needs a stable snapshot.” — platform: all.
6. Every surface that touches a screen edge owns its safe-area inset inside its own background, and the POS root never pads one: each panel's content (products on `bg-background`, the cart, checkout and receipt on `bg-card`) is an `EdgeSurface` on the top and bottom edges (`@wcpos/components/edge-surface`, which replaced `EdgeColumn` in card 18) that pads both insets inside its colour, so the columns and the resize handle run the full screen height and the status bar and home indicator float over the column they sit on (Apple: backgrounds edge to edge, content inside the safe area). The phone layouts pad the status bar on the `bg-background` tab container and the home indicator inside the tab bar; the upgrade notice pads the status bar inside its own colour and zeroes the top inset for the stack below it; the desktop title-bar strip (not a system bar) stays on the root — evidence: owner, iPad landscape, 2026-10-14: flat grey bands under the status bar and the home indicator across the full width; `edge-surface/index.test.tsx`, `pos-columns.test.tsx` — platform: iOS and Android (insets are zero on web; the desktop strip is unchanged).

7. Show the receipt column for the CURRENT order as soon as its stage derives `'receipt'` (a paid checkout member, before confirmation) when receipts show automatically, and keep the same keyed `CheckoutColumn` mounted at receipt stage when they do not — a paid sale must never hand the products column back mid-sale, and the tender's foreground subscriber must stay alive to select or finish — evidence: `pos-columns.tsx` (`receiptUuid`, `showTender`); `pos-columns.test.tsx` 'shows the current paid order its receipt column before any selection' / 'keeps the tender mounted across the paid write when receipts are off' — platform: all, wide layout. _(added 2026-10-23 with the landing PR)_
8. The products column's edge facing the cart keeps no inset: the resize handle is the gutter, one page gutter wide with the grip centred (panels ledger line 5), so the cart's surface (flush with the handle) and the products card sit one page gutter apart, the Gutters table's gap between two content areas; a full gutter on that edge beside an 8 px handle had put nearly two there. `pos-columns.tsx` provides the cart's side as `GroundInnerEdge` around the products column, beside the handle it places (`@wcpos/components/lib/ground`, read by the products bar, the chip row, the rows, the table surface, the tile ground and the caption inset; since card 18 the bar reads it itself and `useGroundInnerEdge` / `PageBar innerEdge` are gone), so the whole frame moves as one and follows the cart to either side — evidence: owner, 2026-10-23 ("the spacing between the cart and the products panel needs to be smaller, when on the left and on the right"); `lib/ground.test.ts`, core `components/data-table/ground.test.tsx` — platform: desktop and tablet.

9. The phone's Products / Cart tab bar is one module, `apps/main/components/pos-phone-tab-bar.tsx`: the (tabs) navigator draws it as its `tabBar` (`POSNavigatorTabBar`) and the (columns) phone fallback draws it from its own state, with the same testIDs, translated labels, the home indicator padded inside it (an `EdgeSurface` on the bottom edge since card 18) and the surface's native haptic (the tabs layout's own `tabPress` haptic and the fallback's hand-built Pressables are deleted) — evidence: card 13 of the 2026-10-28 component-library review; `apps/main/__tests__/pos-phone-tab-bar.test.tsx` — platform: all.

10. An edge's inset is padded once: an `EdgeSurface` marks the edges it pads for everything inside it, so the products column's `PageBar` and `ListPage` read nothing left to pad and the `insetTop={false}` the POS passed is gone; `ListPage insetBottom={false}` stays for the phone, where the tab bar under the pane pads the home indicator in its own colour. The upgrade notice and the desktop title-bar strip in `(pos)/_layout.tsx` still compute their own top (`edgeTop`, the stack's top inset of 0) — evidence: card 18 of the 2026-10-28 component-library review; test "pads an edge once" in `edge-surface/index.test.tsx` — platform: iOS and Android.

11. The desktop title-bar strip is part of the top edge and padded once: `DesktopWindowChrome` only publishes its height (it no longer adds it to `insets.top`), the POS root is an `EdgeSurface` on the `strip` alone (one ground under the window controls, the register bar portalled into it), and the columns under it pad only the status bar; with the upgrade notice showing, a column on the top edge pads the status bar in the notice's colour, so nothing computes `edgeTop` or overrides the stack's insets any more (supersedes the last sentence of line 10) — evidence: the edge-surface desktop slice of card 18; tests "pads the title-bar strip once" (`edge-surface/index.test.tsx`) and "pads the strip once under a surface" (`apps/main/components/desktop-window-chrome.test.tsx`) — platform: desktop (Electron); not checked live.
12. Amends line 11: the register bar is no longer portalled into the desktop strip; it renders at the top of the cart column on every platform, under the strip the POS root pads, and the strip belongs to the drawer layout's title bar (edge-surface ledger line 7) — evidence: Paul, Electron walk 2, 2026-10-29: the portalled bar showed on every page and doubled the bell on Products — platform: desktop (Electron).
