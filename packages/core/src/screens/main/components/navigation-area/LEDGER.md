**Job:** Provide shared responsive navigation for multi-page areas such as Settings and Store Health.

**Composes:** `@wcpos/components/{button,card,error-boundary,hstack,icon,lib/utils,suspense,text}`.

## Lines

1. Wide layouts show a persistent page rail and redirect the area root to its default page; narrow layouts show a tappable index — preserves the approved desktop versus phone navigation model — evidence: `ea688a87c5 2026-07-16 feat(main+core)!: Settings and Store health drawer areas — the modal retires (#700)`; wide-rail, narrow-list, and default-redirect tests in `index.test.tsx` — platform: all.
2. Narrow leaf pages include a back bar navigating to the area index, including when deep-linked — otherwise there is no in-app route to sibling pages — evidence: “A leaf page (or deep link) on a narrow screen has no rail — the back” / “bar is its only in-app route to the area index and its siblings.” (`components/navigation-area/index.tsx:87–88`) — platform: all.
3. Active pages receive both visual selection and accessibility selection state, with optional icons/badges — navigation must communicate which page is open — evidence: `ea688a87c5 2026-07-16 feat(main+core)!: Settings and Store health drawer areas — the modal retires (#700)`; `69a36ee74c 2026-07-22 feat(settings): redesign settings pages with quiet row-based layout` — platform: all.
4. The page column is the page ground (`bg-background`) in both layouts, and every leaf page is ONE card on it through `AreaPage` (`area-page.tsx`): the heading (`text-xl` semibold, `role="heading"` level 1, optional muted description) inside the card as its first element, a hairline rule, then the body, at one reading width (`max-w-4xl`, centred). `scroll` grows the card with the page; `fill` sizes it to the column so a virtualised list owns its scrolling (Logs) — evidence: owner decision 2026-10-18 from the live mockup `wcpos-handoffs/settings-health-body-2026-10-18/full-page.html` (1440 / 1024 / 768 / 390); tests in `area-page.test.tsx` — platform: all.
5. On `lg` the area has no page bar: the leaf page's own heading is the title, the app rail carries the avatar, and the pages need neither the bell nor the offline badge (owner's call; the POS and the list pages keep theirs). The held-session banner and the upgrade notice render above the area rail, the layout pads the top inset the bar used to, and a cashier sheet reopens after a redirect login so its Add user consumer can claim the result; the phone keeps the bar (menu, avatar, back crumb) — evidence: owner, 2026-10-18; lg tests in `index.test.tsx` — platform: all.
6. On `lg` the redirect-login claim of line 5 is the rail's cashier entry's (`components/cashier-entry`, mounted once per screen tree), so the area layout no longer hosts a sheet of its own (`RedirectReturnCashierSheet` is deleted) — evidence: card 13 of the 2026-10-28 component-library review; `cashier-entry/index.test.tsx` — platform: web.
7. Amends lines 5 and 6: on `lg` the page heading's trailing slot carries the bar's controls (Offline as `<bar>-status`, the bell, the avatar, through `BarControls` with the area's `barTestID`), because the rail lost the avatar and the avatar is the one door to the register (Paul, 2026-10-29); the layout fills `AreaPage`'s `AreaHeadingTrailing` slot (a context of a node, so `area-page.tsx` never imports the cashier entry and every screen test that renders an area page stays light) on `lg` only, so a phone's bar still carries them once. The avatar's sheet opens in the drawer layout's `scene` host, beside the rail, and that entry claims a redirect login — evidence: Codex P2 on #3071; test "on %s, %s carries the avatar and the bell, once" in `index.test.tsx` — platform: all. _(added 2026-10-29)_
