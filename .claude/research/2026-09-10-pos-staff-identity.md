# Prior-art POS staff identity — Square, Shopify POS, Lightspeed X/R, Clover, Toast, Loyverse, Odoo (+ Zettle, SumUp, Erply, Revel)

Research for roadmap#203 (map wcpos/roadmap#202). Facts and citations only until §3; primary sources only (official API references, help centres, source code), read 2026-09-10. Anything I could not follow to a source is marked **unverified**. The question: how each system models **staff as distinct from customers** — the identity record, its endpoints, roles, till sign-in, and which staff member a sale is stamped with.

## 1. Comparison

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **Shopify POS** | `StaffMember` (+ POS-only staff, invisible to the API) | admin account; POS-only staff need no email | **read only**, Plus/Advanced-walled | `Order.staffMember` | **yes** — `LineItem.staffMember` | **yes**, a named permission | 4–6 digit PIN, 5-try lockout; no auto-lock | roles yes (UI), effective perms deprecated |

## 2. Per-system notes

Evidence only — the table above is the comparison and §§3–5 are the distillation. Every line carries its source.

### Square

- `TeamMember`: `id`, **`reference_id`** ("A secondary ID linking the team member to a record in an external system"), `is_owner`, `status`, names, `email_address`, `phone_number`, `assigned_locations`, `wage_setting` ([TeamMember](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/objects/TeamMember)). `assignment_type` is `EXPLICIT_LOCATIONS` or **`ALL_CURRENT_AND_FUTURE_LOCATIONS`** ([RetrieveTeamMember](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/team-api/retrieve-team-member)). Pay is a separate sub-resource with a `version` for optimistic concurrency ([WageSetting](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/objects/WageSetting)).
- The record precedes the account — the seller sets permissions and passcode in Dashboard, "after which Square emails an invitation to join the team and create a Square account" — and there is **no delete**: "You can use the Team API to activate or deactivate team members", so you must "Omit the status filter to make sure that you're retrieving both activated and deactivated team members" ([integration guide](https://developer.squareup.com/docs/team/integration)).
- **Attribution is on the payment**, `Payment.team_member_id` ([Payment](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/objects/Payment)). `Order` has no staff field — `Order.source` is an *application* that "shows the most recent application that touched or modified the order" ([Order](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/objects/Order)) — nor does `OrderLineItem` ([OrderLineItem](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/objects/OrderLineItem)). The practitioner case lives in Bookings, where `AppointmentSegment.team_member_id` is **required** ([AppointmentSegment](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/objects/AppointmentSegment)). Reassignment works only on open Restaurants checks ([manage checks](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/8166-comp-void-and-reassign-checks-with-square-for-restaurants)).
- Three passcode tiers — owner, a shared **team passcode**, a personal "unique, 4-digit passcode" — and Square concedes "A team passcode does not support the ability to track time, sales, or activity by team member"; the per-device timeout "determines how long your point of sale stays unlocked after the last interaction" ([passcodes](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/8357-require-passcodes-at-point-of-sale)).
- **The authorization half has no API**: "Permissions, permission sets, and passcodes cannot be accessed using the Team API" (integration guide), and one `EMPLOYEES_READ` covers names *and* `RetrieveWageSetting`. `Customer` has no `team_member_id` ([Customer](https://developer.squareup.com/reference/square/objects/Customer)); staff discounts fall back to a restricted manual discount or a customer-group rule ([discounts](https://squareup.com/help/us/en/article/3955-create-and-manage-discounts)).

### Shopify POS

- `StaffMember` is "a user account with Shopify admin access for running store operations" — no PIN, no POS role, no retail location, and `StaffMemberPrivateData.permissions` **deprecated with no replacement** ([StaffMember](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/StaffMember), [private data](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/StaffMemberPrivateData)). POS-only staff exist in the product but not the API, and **"POS app-only users don't need an email address"** ([POS-only users](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/staff-management/plus-plan-staff-management/pos-only-staff)). It is read-only — "The API only lets you retrieve information about staff" ([REST User](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-rest/latest/resources/user)) — and `read_users` needs "a finance embedded app or installed on a Shopify Plus or Advanced store"; `currentStaffMember` is the self-scoped read.
- **The best idea in the survey**: the POS extension `Session` exposes both `userId` and `staffMemberId`, the latter "the staff member currently pinned into the POS… may differ from the user ID if the pinned staff member is different from the logged-in user", while `getSessionToken()` stays tied to the **authenticated user** ([Session API](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/pos-ui-extensions/latest/target-apis/standard-apis/session-api)).
- Two-tier attribution: `Order.staffMember`, "The staff member who created or is responsible for the order" ([Order](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/Order)), and `LineItem.staffMember`, "Staff attributed to the line item" ([LineItem](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/LineItem)), written from POS via `cart.setAttributedStaff` / `cart.setAttributedStaffToLineItem` ([Cart API](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/pos-ui-extensions/latest/target-apis/contextual-apis/cart-api)). The label is **"Sold by"**: "You can credit a full sale to one staff member, or credit individual items to different staff members" ([sales attribution](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/order-management/sales-attribution)). Reassignment is a permission — "Manage sales attribution for orders — add/edit/delete staff attribution on completed orders".
- PIN is 4–6 digits, switching by tapping the lock icon, and "After 5 incorrect PIN entries, Shopify POS blocks the next attempt for 30 seconds" ([login](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/login), [PIN](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/manage-your-shopify-pos-pin)); no inactivity auto-lock is documented — **unverified**.
- "You can't assign individual permissions to Point of Sale staff, you need to assign a role" ([POS roles](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/staff-management/pos-roles)); each permission is **tri-state — Allowed / Denied / Approval required** — with a manager authorizing by PIN ([POS permissions](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/your-account/users/roles/permissions/pos-permissions)). Location assignment "only restricts where they can use their PIN" ([manage staff](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/sell-in-person/shopify-pos/staff-management/manage-staff)).

### Lightspeed X-Series

- One `User` holds identity, permission, target and attribution: `account_type` (`cashier`|`manager`|`admin`), `permissions[]`, `roles[]` (`system_role_id` "either '100', '200', or '300'"), `rules`, **`restricted_outlet_ids[]`**, `target_daily`/`weekly`/`monthly`, **`switch_id`** ([List users](https://x-series-api.lightspeedhq.com/reference/listusers)). No home outlet — only outlets a user is *restricted to*. **No create and no update endpoint** exists ([API index](https://x-series-api.lightspeedhq.com/llms.txt)); staff can only be provisioned in the UI.
- `source.author_id` is required on a sale, "The ID of the user (cashier) who created the sale"; per line, `line_items[].source.author_id` is "The ID of the salesperson associated with this line item… **Only needed when this line item is not sold by current login user**", returned as `salesperson_id` ([create a sale](https://x-series-api.lightspeedhq.com/reference/createsale)). Attribution is immutable — void and recreate ([changing the user on a sale](https://x-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/25534315171739-How-to-change-the-user-assigned-to-a-sale)).
- Switching is by **barcode**, under four account-wide settings: never require a password, skip it for barcode switches, **require it when switching *up* in privilege**, or always ([switching security](https://x-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/25534175243419-Configuring-your-user-switching-security-settings)). Identification is forced at a parked sale, a layby, a return or converting a quote, and sign-out does not clock the user out ([user switching](https://x-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/25534037435547-User-switching-in-Retail-POS-X-Series)).
- Registers carry **`ask_for_user_on_sale`** but no assigned user ([registers](https://x-series-api.lightspeedhq.com/reference/listregisters)); the audit log records `user_switching_denied` ([audit events](https://x-series-api.lightspeedhq.com/reference/getauditlogevents)); roles cap discounts with a **maximum discount %** ([roles](https://x-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/25534171377819-Setting-user-roles-and-permissions)). Staff-as-customer is a **duplicate record** — an "Employees" customer group holding a second, unlinked Customer per employee ([employee discounts](https://x-series-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/25534308831259-Creating-employee-discounts)).

### Lightspeed R-Series

- `Employee` is "Biographical and login data for an employee, including rights, roles, and clocked hours": `employeeID`, names, `lockOut`, `archived` ("archived staff don't count toward the account limit"), `contactID`, `clockInEmployeeHoursID`, `employeeRoleID` ("if absent, the employee's own rights apply"), `limitToShopID`, `lastRegisterID` ([Employee](https://developers.lightspeedhq.com/retail/endpoints/Employee/)). Full CRUD; POST/PUT accept **`accessPin`**, write-only, never returned. Contactability is factored into a shared **`Contact`** record — the same shape Customer and Vendor use.
- Per-line attribution: `Sale.employeeID` beside a separate **`tipEmployeeID`** ([Sale](https://developers.lightspeedhq.com/retail/endpoints/Sale/)), and `SaleLine.employeeID`, "the employee responsible for this line" ([SaleLine](https://developers.lightspeedhq.com/retail/endpoints/SaleLine/)). Lightspeed ties the PIN to commission: "we suggest assigning PINs and enabling the **Require PIN Before Each Sale** setting… This will increase your chances of assigning items and charges… to the correct employee in the **Line Employee report**" ([commissions](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009184354-Recording-employee-sales-commissions)).
- `Register` carries **`openEmployeeID`** ([Register](https://developers.lightspeedhq.com/retail/endpoints/Register/)); `EmployeeHours` is a CRUD resource ([EmployeeHours](https://developers.lightspeedhq.com/retail/endpoints/EmployeeHours/)). Two idle timers nobody else ships — *Auto Sign Out Delay* and *Verify PIN Delay* — but a session must first be opened by someone with the **External login** right, so Lightspeed warns to keep such a person on site all day because PIN-only staff cannot reopen a closed session ([roles and access](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/229129608-Setting-up-employee-roles-and-access), [login credentials](https://retail-support.lightspeedhq.com/hc/en-us/articles/229129648-Setting-login-credentials-and-access-settings)). `EmployeeRole` and `EmployeeRight` have **no endpoints** — they exist only as `load_relations`.

### Clover

- `Employee`: `id`, `name` (the only required field), `nickname` (shows on receipts), `customId`, `email`, **`inviteSent`**, **`claimedTime`**, `pin`, `unhashedPin`, `role`, `roles[]`, `isOwner`, `employeeCards[]` ([Employee](https://docs.clover.com/dev/reference/employeegetemployees)). The invite lifecycle is in the data model — a device-only record, optionally invited, becoming a claimed Clover login; unclaimed employees are still fully usable POS identities. Full CRUD, except "can't delete the 'owner' employee" ([delete](https://docs.clover.com/dev/reference/employeedeleteemployee)).
- **`Role` carries no permissions** — exactly `id`, `name`, `systemRole`, `employeesRef[]`, `merchant` ([Role](https://docs.clover.com/dev/reference/rolegetroles)) — so a custom role is a label plus a base system role; and `role` (flat) duplicates `roles[]` with a divergent enum (`OWNER` on POST, absent on GET).
- Self-scoping runs through OAuth: the token response returns an `employee_id` beside `merchant_id`, and some writes require an **`X-Clover-Account-Id`** header naming the acting employee ([FAQs](https://docs.clover.com/dev/docs/faqs)).
- `Order.employee` appears in the update-order body, so order-level reassignment is modelled; `Payment.employee`, `refunds[].employee` and `increments[].employee` exist ([payment](https://docs.clover.com/dev/reference/paygetpayment)), but payment reassignment is allowed only "if the payment is a preauth with `captured` currently set to `null`" ([update payment](https://docs.clover.com/dev/reference/payupdatepayment)). No per-line employee. Every `clover.com/help` page is a client-rendered SPA, so **PIN length and the named role toggles are unverified**.

### Toast

- `Employee` requires `entityType: RestaurantUser` and carries `guid`, `externalId`, `firstName`, **`chosenName`** ("To be used, when appropriate, in place of first name"), `lastName`, `email`, `passcode`, `externalEmployeeId`, `jobReferences[]`, `wageOverrides[]` ([Employee](https://doc.toasttab.com/openapi/labor/tag/Data-definitions/schema/Employee/)).
- **The credential is the access tier**: "Administrative — Email address and password" reaches Toast Web and POS; "Non-administrative — 4+ digit POS access code" reaches POS only; and "If you do not include a `passcode`, the employee can log into Toast Web, but cannot log into a Toast POS device" ([access types](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminToastPosAccessTypes.html)). Passcodes "must be unique among all employees at a single restaurant location… must contain 1 to 8 numeric characters" ([checklist](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/cookbook/apiIntegrationChecklistEmployee.html)) — contradicting that 4+ floor. Changing one needs proof of possession: "you must include the employee's current passcode in the `currentPasscode` value" ([PATCH](https://doc.toasttab.com/openapi/labor/operation/employeesEmployeeIdPatch/)). Scopes are split so a menu integration never sees staff PII: `labor:read` "Allows reading all data **except employees**" ([scopes](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/devguide/apiScopes.html)).
- **The cleanest decomposition of "who"**: `Order.server` (credited), `Check.openedBy` (who started it), `Check.payments[].server` (who took the money), `createdDevice` (which terminal), `source` (which channel) ([Order](https://doc.toasttab.com/openapi/orders/tag/Data-definitions/schema/Order/), [Check](https://doc.toasttab.com/openapi/orders/tag/Data-definitions/schema/Check/)). No per-selection attribution; the only person under a line is `appliedDiscounts[].approver` ([Selection](https://doc.toasttab.com/openapi/orders/tag/Data-definitions/schema/Selection/)). Reassignment is first-class — "Change Server", "You can select any employee who is currently clocked in", and "If the check is paid or closed, it is reopened" ([transferring orders](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminTransferringOrders.html)).
- **`Job` is the permission carrier** — "When you assign a job to an employee, that employee inherits all the permissions that are assigned to the job… you can override specific permissions for an individual employee" ([employee jobs](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/platformEmployeeJobs.html)) — yet exposes no permission fields. ~150 named toggles in nine categories have no API representation: *Mode*, *Additional POS* (Cash Drawer Access, No Sale, **Change Server**, Split Checks), *Manager* (Discounts, Void Items/Orders, Unlinked Refunds, Close Out Day, Cash Drawers Blind/Full), *Restaurant Admin* ([permissions](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminPermissions.html)). Escalation is designed in — without a permission "a coworker who has it can enter their POS access code or swipe their access card"; editing another employee needs "a superset of the permissions assigned to the employee you are trying to edit… You cannot give yourself a permission" ([enterprise editing](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminEditingEmployeeInformationJobsAndPermissionsInEnterprises.html)). Staff purchases are ordinary discounts with an `approver` ([discount reasons](https://doc.toasttab.com/doc/platformguide/adminDiscountReasons.html)).

### Loyverse

- `Employee` is `id` (UUID), `name`, `email`, `phone_number`, `stores` ("The list of store ids where this employee has access"), `is_owner`, timestamps — **no PIN, no role, no rights** ([API-Reference v1.0](https://developer.loyverse.com/docs/API-Reference__v1.0.yaml)). Read-only, and the permission table lists **`EMPLOYEES_READ` with no write counterpart**, unlike `CUSTOMERS_WRITE`/`ITEMS_WRITE` (ibid.).
- `Receipt.employee_id` is order-level attribution; `RefundReceipt` carries its own, so the refunder is recorded separately from the seller; and **`PostReceipt` accepts `employee_id`**, so an integration writing a receipt names the employee outright (ibid.).
- `Shift` is first-class and unusually complete: `store_id`, `pos_device_id`, `opened_at`/`closed_at`, **`opened_by_employee`** and **`closed_by_employee`** as separate fields, `starting_cash`, and cash movements each with their own `employee_id` and `PAY_IN`/`PAY_OUT` type (ibid.).
- "Every Loyverse POS user — owner or employee — must have a unique 4-digit PIN that identifies them on the POS" ([PIN access](https://help.loyverse.com/help/pin-code-access)); Employee Management is paid. When an employee hits a function they lack, "the PIN panel will be opened… a one-time access is granted" against a superior's code. Rights are four presets over a named list including *View all receipts* (off = only the five most recent, quietly throttling refunds of old sales), *Apply discounts with restricted access*, *Change taxes in a sale*, *Perform returns*, *Void saved items*, *View shift report*, *Open cash drawer without making a sale* ([access rights](https://help.loyverse.com/help/how-manage-access-rights-employees)).

### Odoo POS (source read on branch `18.0`)

- Base `point_of_sale` has only `pos.order.user_id`, a `res.users` FK labelled "Employee", defaulting to `self.env.uid` ([pos_order.py](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/point_of_sale/models/pos_order.py)). Enable `pos.config.module_pos_hr` and `pos_hr` adds `pos.order.employee_id` → `hr.employee`, string **"Cashier"**, plus a stored computed `cashier` Char falling back `employee_id.name or user_id.name` ([pos_hr/pos_order.py](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/pos_hr/models/pos_order.py)). The order carries **both** the token holder and the cashier, plus a denormalised name snapshot.
- `pos.session` also gains `employee_id` ("The employee who currently uses the cash register", `tracking=True`) — switching cashier does not close the session, and closure aggregates by person via `_aggregate_payments_amounts_by_employee`, bucketing unattributed rows under "Others" ([pos_hr/pos_session.py](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/pos_hr/models/pos_session.py)).
- The PIN is `hr.employee.pin`, a plaintext `Char` whose only constraint is "The PIN must be a sequence of digits" — **no length rule** ([hr_employee.py](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/hr/models/hr_employee.py)). The till receives `hashlib.sha1(pin).hexdigest()` and verifies **client-side**, `employee._pin !== Sha1.hash(inputPin)` ([pos_hr/hr_employee.py](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/pos_hr/models/hr_employee.py), [select_cashier_mixin.js](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/pos_hr/static/src/app/select_cashier_mixin.js)). The typed PIN also *identifies* — a single hash match signs that person in without them naming themselves. Only `['name', 'user_id', 'work_contact_id']` reach the till, narrowed to `basic_employee_ids` ("If left empty, all employees can log in to PoS") plus `advanced_employee_ids` plus the current user ([pos_config.py](https://github.com/odoo/odoo/blob/18.0/addons/pos_hr/models/pos_config.py)).
- **Staff-as-customer is native and free**: `hr.employee.work_contact_id` is a `res.partner`, and `res.partner` is exactly what `pos.order.partner_id` ("Customer") points at — one person, two roles, one contact row. An employee an open session might use cannot be deleted.

### Zettle, SumUp, Erply, Revel

- **Zettle** has no staff record in the API, only an attribution: a purchase carries top-level `userDisplayName`, `userId` and `organizationId`, and a refund carries its own pair ([fetch a purchase](https://developer.zettle.com/docs/api/purchase/user-guides/fetch-purchases/fetch-a-purchase), [with refund](https://developer.zettle.com/docs/api/purchase/user-guides/fetch-purchases/fetch-purchase-with-refund)). Staff are separate email accounts who "have access to the PayPal Point of Sale app but not my.zettle.com", with their name printed on the receipt ([My staff](https://www.zettle.com/gb/help/articles/1084768-my-staff)). **No PIN switching** — every handover is a full sign-out. **SumUp** keeps staff out of the public API entirely ([authorization](https://developer.sumup.com/tools/authorization/authorization)); they live in the back office behind a "User authentication" toggle with "a unique 4-digit password" and per-action ticks ([team member profiles](https://help.sumup.com/en-IE/articles/5GP6wZPslhYcoK5oyEavXi-create-team-member-profiles-pos-pro)).
- **Erply** answers the token-holder problem head-on. `switchUser` "allows you to log in with user PIN (no user name / password needed)" but "can only be used for 'user switching'" — it needs an existing session key, or one expired less than two hours ago, and "Successful user switch will give you a **NEW session key, with the user rights of the PIN holder**", returning `userID`, `employeeID`, `groupID` and a configurable `sessionLength` ([switchUser](https://learn-api.erply.com/requests/switchuser)). Employee and login are explicitly separate: `getEmployees` returns `employeeID`, `jobTitleID`, **`drawerID`**, `warehouses[]`, `pointsOfSale` — and `userID`/`username`/`userGroupID` only "If employee does not have user account, this attribute is not returned" ([getEmployees](https://learn-api.erply.com/requests/getemployees)). Rights are read separately ([getUserRights](https://learn-api.erply.com/requests/getuserrights)).
- **Revel** splits the two identities in the URI — a webhook payload carries `"created_by": "/enterprise/User/4/"` next to `"employee": "/resources/Employee/34/"` and a `role_name` ([webhooks](https://developer.revelsystems.com/revelsystems/docs/webhooks)) — with a dedicated Role endpoint ([Find Role by ID](https://developer.revelsystems.com/revelsystems/reference/getrolebyid)) and a read-only `User` resource ([FAQ](https://developer.revelsystems.com/revelsystems/docs/frequently-asked-questions)). Two permission systems run side by side: POS Role Permissions gate whether the employee can ring at all ("a chef can clock in and out but cannot log into the POS"), while Management Console permission sets are assigned separately with their own username and password ([administrator permissions](https://support.revelsystems.com/hc/en-us/articles/208096886-Setting-up-Administrator-Permissions)). The POS PIN is six digits ([employee management](https://support.revelsystems.com/s/employee-management)); several Revel pages are JS-rendered, so those details are **partly unverified**.

## 3. Convergences — what everyone does

1. **Staff and customer are different resources, and nobody links them.** Square, Shopify, Clover, Toast, Lightspeed X and Loyverse all have zero foreign key between the two. Odoo is the sole exception, and only because `hr.employee.work_contact_id` and `pos.order.partner_id` happen to be the same `res.partner` — which is why Odoo alone gets employee-as-customer for free while Lightspeed X tells merchants to type each employee in twice.
2. **No first-class "staff discount" exists anywhere.** Every system routes it through the ordinary discount machinery — a restricted manual discount (Square), a customer group (Lightspeed X), a discount reason with an `approver` (Toast). The convergence is that a staff *purchase* is a customer with a discount, not a staff-specific object.
3. **Deactivate, never delete.** Square has no delete endpoint at all; Clover refuses to delete the owner; R-Series has `archived` ("archived staff don't count toward the account limit"); Loyverse and Lightspeed expose `deleted_at`; Odoo blocks deleting an employee a live session might use; Toast requires closing open time entries first. Historical sales point at the record, so it never goes away.
4. **The staff record precedes and outlives the login account.** Square invites a `TeamMember` to create an account; Clover has `inviteSent`/`claimedTime`; Toast makes the *credential* the tier (passcode = POS only, email+password = admin too); Erply returns `userID` only "if employee does not have user account"; Shopify's POS-only staff need no email address. The identity is the employment record; authentication is attached to it.
5. **A short numeric code is the till credential, and it identifies as well as authenticates.** 4 digits at Shopify (4–6), Square, Loyverse and SumUp; 6 at Revel and Erply; 1–8 at Toast. Typing it usually *selects* the person — no username step. Lightspeed X is the one real divergence, using a scannable barcode instead.
6. **Sale-level attribution is universal and is stamped from whoever is at the till, not from the token.** Every system has exactly one "this sale belongs to" field, and it is set automatically to the current cashier.
7. **Staff are scoped to a set of locations, not to one.** `assigned_locations`, `stores[]`, `restricted_outlet_ids[]`, `warehouses[]`/`pointsOfSale`, `limitToShopID`. Square's `ALL_CURRENT_AND_FUTURE_LOCATIONS` is the sharpest version — it stores intent so new stores don't silently drop staff.
8. **Permissions are named sets, never per-person flags.** Roles/permission sets everywhere; Shopify says it outright — "You can't assign individual permissions to Point of Sale staff, you need to assign a role."
9. **The named permissions themselves are strikingly consistent.** Every vocabulary contains, in some spelling: refund/return, discount (often with a cap or a "restricted" tier), void, open the cash drawer without a sale, view reports, edit items, and manage other people's open tickets.
10. **Manager override by code is the standard escape hatch.** Loyverse ("a one-time access is granted"), Toast ("a coworker who has it can enter their POS access code"), Shopify ("Approval required" plus a manager PIN), Lightspeed X (a password is always required when switching *up*). A blocked action is a prompt for a superior's credential, not a dead end.
11. **The permission model is the part vendors do not expose.** Square: "Permissions, permission sets, and passcodes cannot be accessed using the Team API." Clover's `Role` has no permission fields. Toast's `Job` has none. Shopify's `permissions` is deprecated with no replacement. R-Series' `EmployeeRole` has no endpoint. Every system lets an integrator read *who*, and none lets it read *what they may do*.

## 4. Divergences — choose with a reason

- **Where the stamp lives.** Square puts it on the `Payment` and leaves `Order` with nothing; everyone else puts it on the order/sale/receipt. Square's choice ties attribution to the tender event, so split tenders, unpaid orders and returns fall outside it. Put it on the order.
- **Per-line "sold by".** Shopify, both Lightspeeds have it; Square, Clover, Toast, Loyverse and Odoo do not. It is the dividing line between retail-with-commission and hospitality. Lightspeed X's phrasing is the one to copy — the line field is "Only needed when this line item is not sold by current login user", so the common case costs nothing and the field is absent rather than redundantly repeated.
- **Reassignment after close.** Shopify makes it a named permission ("add/edit/delete staff attribution on completed orders"); Toast makes it a flow that reopens the check; Clover allows it on the order and, for payments, only while a preauth is uncaptured; Lightspeed X forbids it outright ("void and recreate"); Square allows it only on an open check. This is exactly roadmap#72's ask, and the majority position is that it should be possible and permissioned.
- **Who the drawer session belongs to.** Loyverse splits `opened_by_employee` from `closed_by_employee`; R-Series has `Register.openEmployeeID`; Odoo's session has both a `user_id` and a *current* `employee_id`; Lightspeed X has neither on the register. Two fields plus a mutable "current" is the richest model, and the only one that survives a shift handover.
- **What the number means.** Toast's passcode is a shared secret with proof-of-possession on change (`currentPasscode`); R-Series' `accessPin` is write-only; Clover exposes both `pin` and `unhashedPin`; Odoo ships an unsalted SHA-1 to every browser. Write-only is the right answer.
- **Idle behaviour.** R-Series ships *Auto Sign Out Delay* and *Verify PIN Delay*; Square has a per-device timeout; Shopify documents none at all. A shared till without a timeout keeps the last person's session live indefinitely.

## 5. Mistakes to avoid

- **Do not verify the PIN on the client against a hash you shipped there.** Odoo hands every till an unsalted SHA-1 of a digits-only PIN with no minimum length, and compares in JavaScript. A rainbow table of every 4–6 digit PIN is trivial, and the comparison is bypassable by anyone with devtools.
- **Do not ship a shared code.** Square's own documentation concedes the team passcode "does not support the ability to track time, sales, or activity by team member" — it silently produces sales attributed to nobody while looking like security.
- **Do not couple reading staff to reading pay.** Square's single `EMPLOYEES_READ` covers `RetrieveTeamMember` *and* `RetrieveWageSetting`; Toast avoids this by splitting `labor:read` ("all data **except employees**") from `labor.employees:read`. Split the scope.
- **Do not model a role you cannot read the contents of.** Clover's `Role` is `{id, name, systemRole}` and nothing else; Toast's `Job` exposes no permissions. Any integration must then guess or duplicate the permission model.
- **Do not carry two representations of the same role.** Clover has both `role` (flat enum) and `roles[]` (many-to-many) with a divergent enum between GET and POST; Lightspeed X has `account_type`, `roles[]`, `permissions[]` and `rules` with no documented mapping between them.
- **Do not denormalise the cashier's name onto the order as a stored field.** Odoo's computed `cashier` Char snapshots `employee_id.name` and will drift.
- **Do not make attribution immutable.** Lightspeed X's void-and-re-ring corrupts the register closure the void lands in, and Square's completed payments cannot be fixed at all — so a mis-keyed passcode permanently corrupts commission data.
- **Do not let reassignment silently reopen a closed document.** Toast's "Change Server" reopens a paid check, which its own docs can only warn about.
- **Do not let the identity model live only in the admin UI.** Shopify's POS roles, PINs and POS-only staff have no read path at all, and its POS extensions are left holding numeric ids they may lack the scope to resolve.
- **Do not make till staff dependent on a privileged person being present.** R-Series' PIN-only staff cannot reopen a closed session, so Lightspeed has to advise keeping an External-login holder on site all day.

## 6. WCPOS constraints (one paragraph, per the ticket)

A WCPOS cashier is a WP user with `access_woocommerce_pos`, so the identity record already exists and is already separate from the customer — the convergence in §3.1 is satisfied by accident, and the app's current shape (a `cashier` browse binding that hits the **customers** collection with a client-side `role: { $in: ['administrator','shop_manager','cashier'] }` selector, `packages/core/src/query/query-bindings.ts`) is the wrong half of it: it makes cashiers a *filtered view of customers* rather than a resource in their own right. Free's `wcpos/v1/cashier/{id}` is self-scoped and GET-only, which matches Shopify's `currentStaffMember` and Clover's OAuth `employee_id` pattern precisely, but every surveyed system also ships a manager-scoped *list* — that is the missing endpoint, and it should return the §3.7 field set (id, display name, roles, effective capabilities, accessible stores as a **set** with an all-current-and-future option, active flag) rather than a WP user dump. The existing `_pos_user_created` (write-once) / `_pos_user` (reassignable) pair is already the correct two-field answer to §4's "rung up by vs sold by" and to roadmap#72, and it puts WCPOS ahead of Square and Lightspeed X on reassignment; what it lacks is the permission gate Shopify attaches to that operation, and a per-line variant if commission is ever in scope. Paul's wish to keep cashiers selectable as the customer in the cart is well-precedented — it is exactly Odoo's `work_contact_id` — but it should become an explicit link, not a side-effect of the two collections being the same endpoint. Finally, `next`'s ledger rows already carry `cashier_id` and `store_id`; adding `opened_by`/`closed_by` to the shift (Loyverse) and an `opened_by` to the register (R-Series) costs two columns and is the difference between a closure that survives a handover and one that does not.
